HOW IT WORKS

Your computer, running somewhere else.

Instead of relying only on the hardware inside the device in front of you, AXEN Virtual Computer is designed to run your virtual PC in the cloud and stream the experience to your device.

01

Sign in

Open AXEN Virtual Computer and sign into your AXEN account.

02

Start your PC

AXEN starts your assigned virtual computer in the cloud.

03

Connect

Video and audio stream to your device, while your keyboard and mouse input are sent back to the virtual PC.

04

Keep working

Your files and environment remain associated with your AXEN virtual computer.
